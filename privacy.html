This privacy policy explains which personal data is processed when you visit pharelljay.me. In short: there are no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. However, to display fonts, icons and my current GitHub statistics, your browser connects to a few external services, which are listed below.

2. Hosting on GitHub Pages

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit the site, GitHub processes technically necessary data, in particular your IP address, the date and time of access, the requested file and information about your browser and operating system, in order to deliver the website and to protect the service against abuse.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; my legitimate interest lies in providing the website securely and reliably. Data may be transferred to the USA in the process. GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, for which an adequacy decision of the European Commission exists (Art. 45 GDPR). For details, see GitHub's privacy statement.

3. Fonts (Google Fonts)

This website uses the fonts “Geist” and “Geist Mono”, which are loaded from Google Fonts, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. To display the fonts, your browser connects to Google's servers (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). Google thereby receives your IP address and the information that this website was accessed. Data may also be transferred to Google LLC in the USA; Google is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; my legitimate interest lies in a consistent and legible presentation of the website. For details, see Google's privacy policy and the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

4. Icons (Devicon via jsDelivr)

The technology logos in the “Stack” section come from the open-source icon set Devicon, which is loaded from the content delivery network jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net). jsDelivr is operated by Prospect One sp. z o.o., Kraków, Poland, and uses the networks of several providers, some of which are based in the USA. When the icons are loaded, your IP address and technical request data are transmitted to jsDelivr.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; my legitimate interest lies in the reliable and fast delivery of the icons. For details, see the jsDelivr privacy policy.

5. GitHub profile card

To show up-to-date information about my GitHub profile (public repositories, contributions, last activity and profile picture), your browser retrieves publicly available data directly from the following services when you open the page:

  • GitHub (api.github.com and github.com, for profile data and the profile picture), operated by GitHub, Inc. (see section 2)
  • github-contributions-api.jogruber.de, an independent third-party service that provides the number of GitHub contributions

These services thereby receive your IP address and technical request data. No other information about you is sent. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; my legitimate interest lies in presenting my current work on GitHub.

6. Storage in your browser

To avoid repeating these requests on every visit (and to stay within GitHub's rate limits), the retrieved public GitHub data is cached in your browser's local storage for five minutes under the key gh-profile-v4. It contains only public information about my GitHub profile and no data about you, and it is not transmitted to me or to anyone else. You can delete it at any time in your browser settings.

7. No cookies, no analytics, no tracking

This website does not set any cookies and does not use analytics or tracking tools, advertising or social media plugins. The PDF documents on this website are served directly from this website.

8. Links to other websites

This website contains links to other websites, for example GitHub, LinkedIn and Hochschule Heilbronn. Only when you click such a link does your browser connect to the respective website; from then on, the privacy policy of that provider applies.
